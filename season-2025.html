Paideia Mafia Dispatch

Sons of Ares Take the Crown — Robbie Moore's Second Ring in Three Years

Robbie Moore's Sons of Ares entered the playoffs as the No. 1 seed at 9-5 — the best point differential in the league at +14.5 — and closed the deal, going a perfect 2-0 in the bracket to win it all. It's Moore's second title in three seasons, having also won it in 2023.

A first-round bye sent Sons of Ares straight to the semifinals, where they handled Eli Coppage's Misty Mountain Gools 163.56–132.56. In the final, Robbie held off Gerald Jones' Slim Pickens 137.5–126.26 — a tight, 11.24-point championship game to close the season.

The Regular Season

Three teams tied atop the standings at 9-5 — Sons of Ares, Slim Pickens, and Kript Keepers — with seeding coming down to point differential. The single biggest week belonged to Eli Coppage's Misty Mountain Gools, who dropped 230.36 in Week 12 to demolish Frank Brogden's Kript Keepers 230.36–100.32, a 130-point margin and the most lopsided result of the year. On the other end, Collin Thul's Thul Shed posted the season's low point, just 67.42 in Week 10.

Even the eventual champion wasn't immune to heartbreak: Robbie Moore's Sons of Ares lost the tightest game of the regular season in Week 3, falling to Cameron Kapperman's McYakkin' by just 0.14 points, 125.94–125.80.

Getting There

The other side of the bracket also earned its bye: Slim Pickens rolled through, beating Conner Kapperman's Jehovah's Thiccness 158.38–140.6 to reach the final. Kapperman's squad had been dangerous in Round 1, knocking off Cameron Kapperman's McYakkin' 172.22–128.0, but couldn't get past Slim Pickens.

On the other side of Round 1, Misty Mountain Gools edged Frank Brogden's Kript Keepers 146.7–139.6 — notable since Brogden's squad actually tied for the league's best regular-season record at 9-5, but the tiebreakers left them on the outside of the top seeds looking in.

The Bottom of the Bracket

Misty Mountain Gools bounced back from their semifinal loss to beat Jehovah's Thiccness 114.58–102.38 and claim third place. Down in the true consolation bracket, Alex Evers' Cream Team put together a run, beating The Thul Shed twice — including a nail-biting 129.02–124.18 finish, the closest game of the entire postseason.

At the very bottom, EJ Crutcher's Team Head Bussa's finished the regular season 5-9 — the league's worst record — and closed out the year in the same spot: dead last, losing the final placement game to JaDarion Derrick's D¡ck Diamond 115.98–103.46.
